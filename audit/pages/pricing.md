## Top Three Fixes
1. Remove "Managed SIEM" and "Identity Threat Detection & Response" from the Compliance & Continuity tier everywhere they appear (pricing.html:745, :1659-1704, :2215; JSON-LD :170) - these are the page's clearest house-rule violations, both explicitly excluded or unlisted in VERIFIED FACTS, and both are load-bearing selling points for the top-priced tier, not incidental mentions.
2. Fix the calculator's static/no-JS savings-card numbers (pricing.html:1051-1115) so they match what assets/js/pricing.js actually computes for the page's own default state (10 users, Secure Growth): In-house total should read $8,333/mo (not $31,250), saved $6,583/mo and $78,996/yr at 79% (not $4,667/$56,004 at 73%), with Systems Engineer and Network Engineer shown as "not yet needed at 10 endpoints" rather than active at $10,833 and $12,083. As written, this is inconsistent with itself (the $31,250 hireCost figure doesn't reconcile with the $4,667 savings figure two rows below it) and will mislead anyone or anything reading the raw HTML without running the JS.
3. Add the Microsoft 365 default-scope qualifier and the Google Workspace/Apple-fleet carve-out to every place the $1,500 per-user onboarding figure appears (onboarding card pricing.html:1190-1207, FAQ :2207-2211, JSON-LD FAQ :217-221) so it doesn't read as a flat platform-agnostic price; also get Uli's [VERIFY] sign-off on the unsourced $2,500/$4,000 onboarding tiers, the 12-month/+15%/50%-cancellation contract terms, and the full hourly-rate table (pricing.html:2085-2145), none of which appear in VERIFIED FACTS.
