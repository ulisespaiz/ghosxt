Onboarding and offboarding for distributed and contractor teams

The recurring pain point for a Google Workspace and Mac shop is not the steady state, it is the churn: a new hire starts, a contractor rolls off, and the question is whether access actually gets granted or revoked correctly, every time, regardless of where the person sits.

A new employee or contractor is provisioned the same way whether they sit in a California office or work remotely: a Mac enrolled through Apple Business Manager, a Google Workspace account created with access scoped to what the role needs, MFA enrollment before first login, and any shared credentials placed in the enterprise password vault rather than sent over chat.

Distributed and contractor fleets, including remote and international contractors such as those based in India, run under the same identity and device policy as any other seat: Google Workspace as the identity provider and phishing-resistant MFA do not change based on geography, and a Mac managed through Apple Business Manager can be remotely locked or wiped the same way no matter where it physically sits. Offboarding is planned as part of the same lifecycle: Google Workspace access suspended and the device managed out of the fleet when a contract or role ends.

If your customers require a Data Processing Agreement that flows security obligations down to your vendors and contractors, the identity, device management, and monitoring controls described on this page are generally the technical backbone that kind of agreement expects. Review your specific DPA language with your customer and your own counsel; we support the technical controls, not the legal review.

FAQs about Google Workspace and Mac managed IT

Do you support companies that run Google Workspace instead of Microsoft 365?
Yes. We run Google Workspace as the identity provider, with phishing-resistant MFA enforced on every account, cloud backup covering your Gmail and Drive data, and the same managed detection and response with a 24/7 SOC, OS and third-party patching, DNS and web filtering, and security awareness training we run for Microsoft 365 environments.
Can you manage an all-Mac fleet?
Yes. We use Apple Business Manager for zero-touch enrollment, so a new Mac can ship straight to a new hire or contractor and enroll into management on first boot. Patching, monitoring, managed detection and response, and the enterprise password vault apply to Mac endpoints the same way they do on Windows.
We hire contractors, including some outside the US. Does that change the setup?
Not on the security policy. Google Workspace as your identity provider and phishing-resistant MFA apply the same way no matter where a sign-in originates, and a Mac enrolled through Apple Business Manager is managed the same way whether it ships domestically or to a contractor working from another country, including India. Onboarding and offboarding steps for a distributed or contractor fleet are scoped as part of your Google Workspace and Apple onboarding engagement rather than assumed.
Our customer contract requires a Data Processing Agreement. Does your setup help with that?
Often, yes, at a high level. A Data Processing Agreement your customer asks you to sign typically expects identity controls, device management, and monitoring on any system that can reach their data, which lines up with the stack on this page. Review the specific DPA language with your customer and your own counsel; we support the technical controls, we do not provide the legal review.
