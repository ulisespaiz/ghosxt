Five things worth trying first

None of these are required. Call us any time. But these five resolve a surprising share of what comes in, and you might be back to work in ninety seconds.

  • Restart the machine, fully. Not sleep, not close the lid. Shut down and power back on.
  • Check whether it is only you. If a coworker has the same problem, say so when you call. It changes the diagnosis immediately.
  • Sign all the way out of Microsoft 365. For Microsoft 365 and Outlook, sign out completely and sign back in before assuming the account is broken.
  • Confirm the network. For printers and Wi-Fi, confirm you are on the office network and not a guest network or a phone hotspot.
  • Capture the exact error. Write down the exact error text or take a photo of the screen. The wording matters more than the description.

When it needs hands on it

Most support issues are solved remotely, but some things need a person standing in front of the hardware: a dead switch, a failed workstation, a new-hire desk setup, a network drop. We go on-site across Salinas, Monterey, Watsonville, Santa Cruz, Hollister, Seaside, Marina, Pacific Grove, Carmel, Gilroy, and San Jose. Outside that radius, remote support covers the rest of the country the same way it covers most issues here: fast, and without anyone needing to be in the room.

Support FAQs

Who actually answers when I call?
The engineer who runs your environment. There is no tier-one layer, no offshore queue, and no script. You get the same person each time, which means you never re-explain your setup.
What counts as an emergency?
Anything that stops the whole business: systems down, a server offline, email dark, files encrypted, or a suspected breach. Those get triaged ahead of everything else. If you are unsure, call and say what is happening. We would rather sort it out with you than have you sit on it.
What happens outside business hours?
Monitoring and the 24/7 security operations center keep running and escalate critical security events regardless of the hour. Routine requests filed overnight are picked up at the start of the next business day.
Do I have to use the portal, or can I just call?
Call. The portal exists so nothing gets lost and so you can see status on your own, not to put a form between you and a person.
How do I start a remote session?
Call (831) 204-0501 first. On the call, the engineer gives you a short session code. Click the Start remote session button on this page (or go to support.ghosxt.com), enter the code, and run the small file that downloads. You watch everything, and the session ends when the call does.
Can you take over my screen without asking?
No. Remote sessions are started with you on the call and end when the call ends. You watch the whole thing.
I am not a client. Can I still get help?
For a one-off, yes, at the published hourly rate on our pricing page. If it keeps happening, a managed plan is almost always cheaper than paying by the incident.
